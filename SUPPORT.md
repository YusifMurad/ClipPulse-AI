## FAQ quick links

- **Is it free?** Yes, MIT licensed.
- **Where do I get a Gemini key?** https://aistudio.google.com/apikey (free)
- **My YouTube download fails (403)?** Make sure Deno is installed and on `PATH`
  (`export PATH="$HOME/.deno/bin:$PATH"`). ClipPulse invokes yt-dlp with the Deno JS runtime.
- **API key not saving after refresh?** It is masked in the UI by design; the real
  key stays server-side. Just reload — it's still there.
